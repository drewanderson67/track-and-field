How to Play

Hammer the two RUN buttons alternately — left, right, left, right. Hitting the same button twice in a row barely counts. Use ACTION to jump or throw; for long jump and javelin hold ACTION to raise the angle and release to launch (around 40–45° is ideal).

Meet the qualifying mark in every event to advance. Clear all six and the next round starts with tougher standards. Miss one and it's game over.

Keyboard: ← → or A D to run, Space / ↑ / W for action, Esc to pause. Gamepad: LB/RB or X/B to run, A for action.
